Thank you for helping us build GSUSA by TAZ around how your cookie and shop orders actually move. This page takes about five minutes: download the guide and template, export your package-level data, and upload it below. Files go straight to Xander at HTL by email; nothing is stored on this page.

1Download the guide and template

The guide shows what to export from NetSuite or your carrier accounts. The template shows the columns we need. Your own export format is fine too.

2What to export

  1. Date range: the last 12 months if you can, including a full cookie season (January–April).
  2. From NetSuite: a saved search of item fulfillments with ship date, ship-from location, ship-to ZIP and state, carrier, service, package weight and dimensions.
  3. Or from your carriers: the shipment or invoice detail export from UPS, FedEx, USPS, or your shipping platform.
  4. Leave out customer names, street addresses, emails, and phone numbers. ZIP and state are all we need.
  5. CSV or Excel are best. Several files are fine; up to five at a time.
